# Privacy policy — Timer for 9Barista

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

Timer for 9Barista (`com.freedomfighter.baristatimer`) is published by Pierre Gallaz. It times the heating and the extraction of an espresso on a 9Barista machine. It is free, has no ads, no account and no tracking.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library.

## Network use

None. The app has no network permission and never connects to the Internet.

## Data kept on the phone

Nothing is written to the phone. The timer lives only in memory while the app is on screen; closing the app forgets everything. Android backup is disabled for the app.

## Permissions

The app asks only for vibration, so that the phone can buzz at 25 and 30 seconds of extraction and after 8 minutes of heating. It also keeps the screen on while a brew is being timed.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
